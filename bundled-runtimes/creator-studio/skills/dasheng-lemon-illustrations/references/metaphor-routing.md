# Metaphor And Example Routing

## Trigger Decision

Scan the source before rewriting it. Create an illustration intent when the source contains:

- an explicit example: “比如、例如、举个例子、假设一下、想象一下”；
- an explicit metaphor: “就像、好比、仿佛、如同、把……看作”；
- a financial physical metaphor: “抽血、虹吸、踩刹车、开闸、蓄水池、地心引力、接力、吞噬、黑洞”；
- an Agent-detected abstract mechanism that becomes clearer as a physical action.

Keyword matching only creates a candidate. Generate only when the image adds understanding. Skip literal matches, weak clichés, repeated poses, and beats where real evidence is the correct visual.

## Story Grammars

### Literalized Metaphor

Turn the phrase into a physical action. Example: “流动性被抽走” becomes柠檬人 operating a pump that drains water from old asset buckets into one large IPO tank.

### Example Micro-story

Use three beats: setup, action, result. Example: “假设你在高位接盘” becomes柠檬人 stepping onto an upward escalator, the escalator reverses, and the valuation box lands on its shoulders.

### Causal Machine

Use a pipe, gate, relay, conveyor, bridge, or machine. Each component maps to one causal step; do not turn it into a dense formal flowchart.

### Constraint Or Risk

Use weight, bottleneck, leak, pit, narrow bridge, or stuck gear. The character must physically struggle with the constraint.

### Contrast

Use one stable split composition. Keep the character's action different on each side and reveal the consequence without repeatedly cutting between two cards.

### Counterintuitive Twist

Use reversed direction or an unexpected operator action. This works well for “看起来是高切低，实际是低切高” and similar thesis reversals.

## Channel Adaptation

### WeChat Article

- Place the illustration immediately after the paragraph containing the metaphor/example.
- Use one 16:9 white-background image or a 2-3 panel micro-comic.
- A long article normally uses 4-8 high-value illustrations, not one per paragraph.
- Caption is optional and should interpret the image in one line, not repeat the paragraph.

### Talking-head Video

- Short aside: transparent overlay beside the speaker.
- Core metaphor: switch to full-canvas illustration, hide or shrink the speaker, then return to speaker/evidence.
- Animate setup, character action, and result separately; do not zoom one flattened image.
- Use 4-7 seconds for a simple metaphor and 7-12 seconds for an example story.

### No-human Explainer

- Use the comic as a full scene or layered character/prop/path composition.
- End on the result long enough to read, then continue to evidence or the next chapter.
- Never let two consecutive comic scenes become a substitute for the article's real charts or documents.

## Intent Fields

Every intent should eventually contain:

- source paragraph and trigger phrase;
- one-sentence core meaning;
- story grammar;
- one concrete柠檬人 action;
- required props and optional short labels;
- WeChat placement;
- video mode, duration, motion beats, and return target;
- QA risks: cuteness, clutter, repeated pose, collision, or evidence replacement.
